# VECR after the v6 comparison — 28 October 2026

## Candid assessment

The completed work is a technically substantive **systems and empirical contribution**:
a precise assigned-edge obligation contract; bounded acquisition-time evidence
history; exact attempt identity; conditional sensing-envelope recovery; and a
reproducible comparison that separates coverage tracking, metadata waiting and
re-entry conservatism. This supports a focused dissertation chapter and research
artifact. It does **not** establish that returning to a departure point is new,
that the derived rule dominates existing alternatives, or that a dissertation
committee must accept the contribution.

A defensible formulation is: **“Evidence-conditioned execution and recovery of
assigned survey edges: an onboard contract, conditional bounds and comparative
validation.”** The contribution is specified by its assumptions and implementation,
not a claim of worldwide priority for its familiar components. The digital study
shows the proposed envelope target adds travel without better completion than the
strongest simple gap-aware baseline; this negative result belongs in the abstract.

Primary sources were consulted in the 26–28 October review. This is a targeted
comparison, not a systematic exhaustive priority search. It identifies overlap;
it cannot prove absence of any earlier equivalent method.

## What changed after the earlier assessment

The missing resume/rewind comparisons now exist. The new campaign has nine
policies, 22 conditions and 30 paired seeds: 5,940 primary attempts. Delayed and
reordered evidence, multiple gaps, varying bounds and unsuccessful recovery are
measured. The extra GW comparator isolates waiting from target selection. It is
at least as successful as E and often cheaper; concealing it would undermine the
research. The 200 client-reuse attempts match across the two SDK entry paths.

The defensible advance is therefore a documented implementation and controlled
characterization with a modest conditional result, **not an experimentally superior
new optimizer**. Native uncertainty qualification and real image-quality evidence
remain separate deployment work. SITL adds integration evidence, not novelty.
A stronger algorithmic dissertation claim would need a genuinely different
technical result and new preregistered evidence; neither should be asserted from
these data. Degree sufficiency depends on the institution, supervisor, publications
and the complete thesis, which this experiment cannot determine.
