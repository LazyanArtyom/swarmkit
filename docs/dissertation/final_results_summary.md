# SwarmKit state acceptance — final results summary

Date: 20 August 2026

Primary environment: deterministic `SimBackend`, 3 UAVs

Campaign: 50 replicate clusters × 9 scenarios × 100 scored requests = 45,000 requests per method

This document reports values generated by the frozen protocol in
`final_experiment_protocol.md`. The machine-readable sources of record are the files in
`results/dissertation/`; the values below were not selected as optimization targets.

## Main semantic result

The common accepted-output oracle checks 3D position error at the physical evaluation time,
LocalNED frame, current selected-evidence incarnation, and only the health, mission,
provenance, and GPS predicates declared by the fixed contract. It does not introduce a
separate current simulator-health predicate.

| Timestamp-aligned + age (`B1`) | 45,000 | 39,125 | 23.387% [23.349%, 23.426%] | 86.944% [86.798%, 87.084%] | 20.333% [20.333%, 20.333%] |
| Proposed state acceptance (`P`) | 45,000 | 22,981 | 0.000% [0.000%, 0.000%] | 51.069% [50.891%, 51.236%] | 0.000% [0.000%, 0.000%] |

The paired difference `FV_P - FV_B1` was -23.387 percentage points, with a 95% replicate-cluster
bootstrap interval of [-23.426, -23.349] percentage points. The descriptive relative reduction
was 100%. No p-value was computed or reported.

No false-valid events were observed for `P` in this campaign. This finite result applies to the
configured scenarios and assumptions; it does not establish a universal failure rate.

## Scope and limitations

- `SimBackend` is the primary theorem-conformance environment in this campaign.
- Finite results do not establish a universal failure rate.
- Deterministic guarantees depend on valid hard upstream observation, speed, and clock bounds.
- The macOS ARM64 benchmark does not establish target embedded real-time performance.
- Unsigned SHA-256 consistency hashes bind serialized content but do not establish adversarial
  authenticity.
- Component-wise spatial enclosures do not by themselves prove arbitrary joint cross-UAV
  correlation properties.
