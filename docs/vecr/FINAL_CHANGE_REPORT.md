# VECR v8 final change and gate report — 29 October 2026

## Implemented method

SwarmKit now exposes one production edge-execution method. It monitors a caller-assigned straight edge, qualifies survey and traversal evidence, detects sustained in-flight corridor deviation, selects the earlier of projected progress and the earliest required gap for bounded local re-entry, verifies physical stopped re-entry, and resumes toward B. On qualified stopped B arrival it waits at most the configured evidence-finalization period, then returns `ARRIVED` with an independent coverage verdict and unresolved intervals. It never issues an autonomous gap-repair command from B. The non-installed B2 binary exists solely as a fair full-restart research comparator.

The public C++/protobuf state model, Agent, SDK, CLI and examples use the same `MOVING`, `DEVIATING`, `RECOVERING`, `ARRIVED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, `UNKNOWN` vocabulary. `GetEdgeStatus` is an exact-attempt pull snapshot; `StartEdgeResults` gives terminal-only callbacks with per-live-subscription deduplication. Cancellation and failed local recovery have distinct results. Journal-backed status supports reconciliation after disconnect; exactly-once network delivery across crashes is not claimed. Recovery has finite attempt, duration and conservative travel limits. The agent requests a backend hold on terminal failure where supported and keeps physical-navigation uncertainty explicit until stopped evidence is available.

## Experimental execution and validation

The source/protocol commit `66603912556002d162dc42d910b598bfbbc480a2` produced the frozen **1,320-attempt** v8 primary campaign: N00–N21, 30 common seeds per condition, VECR and B2. Its manifest records binary hashes and all assignments. Four 88-attempt pilots are separate. Independent geometric validation passed all 1,320 attempts, 54,850 delivered captures and 180 re-entry dwells. Across **all 1,320 attempts**, 1,080 reached qualified B arrival and 540 reported complete surveys. Among the **1,200 honest-sensor attempts**, 960 arrived, 420 reported complete, and 540 were independently true complete; there were zero false reported completions and 120 conservative false negatives from N03/N15. The **120 dishonest controls** all arrived and reported complete despite incomplete truth, yielding 120 deliberate false reported completions. In the three jointly successful in-flight recovery conditions N07–N09, VECR saved 24.933, 25.219 and 23.478 m per attempt versus B2 respectively, with pointwise paired 95% bootstrap intervals in `primary/analysis/paired_comparisons.csv`. These results support conditional flight-distance efficiency in this simulator; they do not establish a completion advantage or real-flight safety.

On 30 October 2026, a post-commit audit corrected a reader/prose denominator error: 960 arrivals and 420 reported completions are honest-subset totals, not full-campaign totals. The full-campaign totals are 1,080 and 540. No experiment or frozen result file was modified; the website now computes its headline values from raw attempt rows and validates them.

The separately frozen qualified reuse campaign exercised direct `Client` and routed `SwarmClient` paths in N00/N01/N04/N07/N11: 100 attempts, 50 matched pairs, zero differences across ten audited fields. Independent validation passed 100 attempts, 4,054 captures and 20 re-entry dwells. An earlier 100-attempt reuse execution failed because the sandbox denied local loopback bind; it remains under `results/vecr/v8/reuse/`, sealed and excluded from qualified parity statistics. The qualified data are under `results/vecr/v8/reuse-qualified/`.

Release CTest rerun: **3/3 passed in 20.46 s** (`swarmkit_evidence_inspect_help`, `swarmkit_tests`, `swarmkit_vecr_baseline_contract`). The 20-chapter website has a reproducible builder, cross-checks its tables/replays against raw v8 records, and passed static plus desktop/mobile headless Chrome validation. The website and all three v8 research directories have SHA-256 inventories. `EXPERIMENT_REVALIDATION.md` maps every active table and figure to its raw inputs; `TRACEABILITY.md` maps scientific claims to source and tests.

## Removed from active claims

Automatic post-B gap repair, the obsolete v7 completion/distance charts, old endpoint-repair animations, proposed-only status labels and stale v7 metadata no longer appear as current results. V7 raw data, SITL records and prior site drafts remain clearly labelled as historical. There is no rotor-router or gossip implementation in SwarmKit.

## Remaining qualification

The v8 campaign is a **single-vehicle-per-attempt, agent-backed geometric digital simulation** with a flat rectangular camera model and synthetic declared hard bounds. It has an independent simulator oracle, not real calibrated imagery. The client-path comparison does not test two graph algorithms or distributed fleet execution. Pointwise confidence intervals are conditional on the simulator design and selected joint-success pairs. The 12 archived ArduPilot SITL flights used v7 and synthetic sensing; **revised-method v8 SITL is not run and all such numbers are TBD**. Image-pixel quality, obstacle-aware flight, swarm collision behavior, hardware airworthiness and field coverage remain unvalidated.
